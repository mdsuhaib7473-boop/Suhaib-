Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a cup of flour weigh less than a cup of sugar?

Because they have different densities — flour is light and airy while sugar is more compact, so the same volume weighs a different amount.

Is weighing more accurate than using cups?

Yes, especially for baking — a kitchen scale removes the variation caused by how ingredients are packed or leveled.
